# MSCI World Factor Strategy

**Status: In development**

A university research project investigating an MSCI World factor strategy and the economics of a potential fund concept.

## Research

The project brings together two complementary studies:

- **Investment backtest:** historical portfolio performance, factor allocation, leverage, financing and transaction costs, benchmarks and risk.
- **Capital inflow study:** hypothetical investor subscriptions, ownership, fees, redemptions and operating costs.

The investment study and the fund economics model answer different questions. Hypothetical inflow scenarios are not forecasts derived from historical investment returns.

## Planned publication

The planned release will include selected reports, methodology, model code, validation and instructions for reproducing the research with appropriately obtained source data. Assumptions and scope remain under development.
